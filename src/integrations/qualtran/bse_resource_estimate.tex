\section*{Setup and method}
Multi-exciton BSE effective Hamiltonian; Coulomb / screened Coulomb via ISDF--THC; direct + exchange
terms. Diamond, $6{\times}6{\times}6$ $k$-mesh ($N_k=216$), point group $|P|=48$, $n_{\rm ao}=26$/cell.
Rank $c5\Rightarrow n_{\rm IP}=136$; $c8\Rightarrow n_{\rm IP}=208$.
All counts from the analytic cost model \code{XPRIZE\_paper/script/\_resource\_dispatch.py} at
angle precision $b=32$, \code{mode="T-opt"} (per-QROAM select--swap $\lambda$ chosen for minimum
Toffoli). Primitives used: P-06 \code{unitary\_synthesis}, P-07 \code{forward\_isometry\_synthesis},
P-09 \code{diagonal\_matrix\_block\_encoding}, P-12 \code{eigendecomposition\_block\_encoding}
(ledger \code{XPRIZE/context/primitives.md}).

\textbf{Operations} (per the model, $N_k$ carried as the QROAM address prefix):
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep0pt
\item \textbf{Exchange} $\to$ eigendecomposition BE of $W$: $216$ blocks of $n_{\rm IP}\times n_{\rm IP}$.
\item \textbf{Direct} $\to$ diagonal BE of $W$: $216\times n_{\rm IP}^2$.
\item Occupied $X$: $6\times$ isometry $n_{\rm IP}\times4$ (216 blocks).
\item Virtual $X$: $6\times$ isometry $n_{\rm IP}\times22$ (216 blocks).
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Symmetry-adapted construction}
Replace $X,W$ by their irrep-block structure; add \textbf{6 $Q$'s} (generalized-Fourier / symmetry
adaptation, reused across grid legs) + register-recording (family/coset canonicalization). In the
symmetry basis both $W$'s are diagonalized, \emph{but the eigenvector unitary must still be built}:
\[
   W \;=\; U\,D\,U^\dagger,\qquad U=\bigoplus_i \mathbb 1_{m_i}\otimes u_i \ \text{(copy-space, blocks }n_i).
\]
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0pt
\item \textbf{Exchange} $W=2U+D$: $U=$ block \code{unitary\_synthesis} over copy blocks $\{n_i\}$
      (group by size, floor${<}4\!\to\!4$); $D=$ diagonal BE over $\sum n_i$ eigenvalues.
\item \textbf{Direct} $W$: diagonal BE over $\sum n_i^2$ reduced values.
\item \textbf{6 $Q$}: each $=$ orbit synthesis (block \code{unitary\_synthesis} over the orbit histogram)
      $+$ reconstruction $\approx 2770\cdot\tfrac{32}{24}$ (stage-B perm/fold-back, $n_k$-indep).
\item \textbf{$X$}: $6\times$ occ $+\ 6\times$ virt isometries, family-reduced (\code{forward\_isometry\_synthesis}$(n_{\rm IP},M,N_{\rm fam}{=}16,b)$).
\end{itemize}

\textbf{c8 extrapolation:} scale multiplicities $n_i\times r$ and orbit counts $\times r$,
$r=208/136=1.529$ (both consistent with grid dim $\to 216\cdot208=44{,}928$); \#irreps ($57$) and
orbit-size shape held fixed; recompute every cost with the primitives. $X$ computed \emph{exactly} at
$208$. Scaled: $\sum n_i{=}2705$, $\sum n_i^2{=}200{,}735$, $\sum|o|^2{=}20{,}856$.

\section*{Key findings}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0pt
\item \textbf{$W$ collapses, $X$ does not.} $W$ becomes diagonal $\Rightarrow$ data $\to$ eigenvalues
      (direct $W\!\sim\!6$k). $X$ stays an isometry: the $M$-column rotation structure is irreducible, so
      symmetry only shrinks the row/data dimension. $X$ reduces $\sim\!3.4\times$ (from the $k$-family
      fold $216\!\to\!16$) and is the \textbf{dominant} symmetry-adapted term ($\sim48\%$).
\item \textbf{Eigenvector unitary $U$ is real cost.} $W=UDU^\dagger$ needs $U,U^\dagger$; $2U$ is the
      2nd-largest term ($\sim33\%$). (An earlier draft wrongly dropped it to just $D$.)
\item \textbf{6 $Q$'s are cheap} ($\sim18\%$): the true orbits are mostly tiny (104 size-1, 398 size-2).
\item \textbf{Block isometry $\not<$ dense-per-family.} Summing the 49 \code{grid-ao} blocks
      independently (425k c5 / 503k c8) \emph{loses amortization} and beats neither; the dense-16-family
      form is used. A proper shared-column-loop block synthesis (P-15/16, loop to $\max_i n_{\rm band}$)
      could trim $X$ to $\sim\!250$--$350$k (total $\sim\!740$--$780$k), not computed here.
\end{itemize}

$Q_{\rm orbit}={\sum}$\code{unitary\_synthesis}(orbit sizes grouped); $X=$\code{forward\_isometry\_synthesis}($n_{\rm IP}$,M,16,b).
Structure: \code{log\_irreps/data\_ov\_diamond\_6x6x6\_c5\_grid-grid.txt} ($n_i$),
\code{...\_grid-ao.txt} ($X$ blocks), \code{diamond\_symm\_orbit\_data.json} (orbits).

\textbf{Caveats:} c5 exact, c8 extrapolated; occ/virt split of $n_{\rm ao}$ taken $4{:}22$ proportional;
$X$ uses dense-per-family (not the shared-loop block form); reconstruction is a scaled estimate; the
whole layer is the analytic model, not an executed circuit.
